THE CODE OF THE ARENA

Play by the rules.

Fair matches make a stronger community. Check each tournament's custom instructions before joining.

01

Correct IGN

Register using the exact Free Fire in-game name you will use in the match.

02

Respect timing

Join the published room on time and follow the organiser's lobby instructions.

03

Fair play only

No cheats, exploits, teaming outside the format, harassment, or deliberate match disruption.

04

Protect your account

Never share your password, login codes, or OTP. BattleForge will not ask for them.

05

Read match notes

Every tournament may have its own map, schedule, scoring, lobby, and disqualification rules.

06

Results & disputes

Follow the organiser's instructions for screenshots, score reporting, and result review.

Tournament registrations and results are managed online. Wallet funds are credited only after verification; withdrawals are reviewed and paid manually.
