Information we process

  • Account and identity details: sign-in email and authentication identifiers, username, BattleForge player ID, account timestamps and selected preset avatar.

  • Support and safety: support-ticket messages, match disputes, moderation records, warnings, bans and reports.
  • Device and service data: push-notification device tokens, platform information, basic technical logs and diagnostics needed to operate and secure the app.

Why we use it

To create and secure accounts; operate tournament registrations and results; maintain player IDs, leaderboards and referrals; review payment requests and maintain accurate wallet records; send match and service notifications; answer support requests and disputes; enforce fair-play rules; prevent fraud and abuse; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Service providers and sharing

Depending on the feature used, data may be processed by Supabase (authentication/database hosting), Cloudflare R2 (configured archive/object storage), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Google sign-in services, and the payment/UPI provider or bank chosen by the player. Telegram support is an external service. We do not sell personal information. We share only what is necessary with service providers, authorised staff, or where legally required. Provider list and processing locations must be confirmed before publication.

Security and retention

We use access controls, database row-level security, restricted administrative permissions and operational safeguards. No internet service can promise absolute security. We retain information only for as long as needed for the stated purposes, dispute handling, security, accounting and legal requirements. A final retention schedule, deletion process and backup-expiry schedule must be approved and published before launch.

2. Terms of service & fair-play rules

Operator: [INSERT LEGAL ENTITY / OWNER NAME AND ADDRESS]. These terms are a draft and become binding only after legal approval, publication of the final version and clear user acceptance.

Eligibility and accounts

  • Use accurate account information and only an account you control. Keep sign-in credentials and OTPs private.
  • One person may not impersonate another player, manipulate referrals, create duplicate accounts to obtain benefits, or transfer/sell an account without written permission.
  • Age, geographic and eligibility restrictions will be shown before registration and must follow applicable law and game/platform rules.

Tournaments and fair play

  • Each tournament page must show its mode, schedule, map/format, entry conditions, scoring, capacity, prize description, result process and organiser instructions before a player joins.
  • Players must use the registered in-game name and follow room instructions. Cheating, exploits, collusion, harassment, match manipulation, false score submissions and disruption are prohibited.
  • BattleForge may investigate credible integrity reports, request relevant evidence, hold a disputed result, correct an error, or restrict an account after a fair review and an opportunity to respond where appropriate.
  • Results should identify the match, scoring basis, publication time and dispute route. No result should be silently changed after publication.

Coins, deposits and withdrawals

The current product design distinguishes deposited balance from earned match balance. Deposited balance is intended for permitted entry fees and is not withdrawable; eligible earned balance may be used for entries or requested for withdrawal. The current app configuration describes a ₹30 minimum deposit, ₹100 minimum withdrawal and 2% withdrawal fee. These are product settings, not a statement that this payment model is lawful.

No paid-entry tournament, cash-equivalent reward, deposit collection or withdrawal service may be offered until an Indian lawyer confirms in writing that the exact BattleForge product, coin design, Free Fire tournament format, prize funding, operator role, payment flows and state-wise availability are lawful and all required recognition/registration and payment arrangements are in place. If counsel concludes the model is prohibited or requires a different structure, the product must be changed before use.

Where a lawful wallet service is approved, each payment page must show the amount, fee, eligibility, processing expectations and dispute route before confirmation. A screenshot alone is not proof of payment. Never send a UPI PIN or OTP to support staff.

Suspension and termination

We may restrict access to protect players, investigate suspected fraud or cheating, comply with law, or respond to security incidents. Decisions affecting balances or prizes must be documented, reviewable and handled under the approved dispute/refund policy. The final appeal process must be published.

3. Tournament cancellation, postponement & refund policy

This draft must be reconciled with the legal classification and payment model before any paid tournament is offered.

Before a tournament starts

  • If BattleForge cancels a tournament, affected players must be told the reason and the available remedy. Any refundable amount should be returned to the original payment method or handled through a legally approved balance reversal; it must not be silently converted into a different product.
  • If a tournament is postponed, players must receive the new date/time and a clear option to accept the change or request the remedy permitted by the final policy.
  • If a player withdraws before the published cut-off, the tournament-specific page must state whether a refund is available and how to request it.

After a tournament starts

If a match is interrupted, compromised or cannot be fairly completed, the organiser must record the incident, preserve available evidence, pause result publication and communicate the review outcome. Any refund, replay or other remedy must follow the tournament's pre-published rules and applicable law. No blanket forfeiture should be applied without a disclosed rule and documented review.

Payment errors

Duplicate payments, incorrect credits, failed transfers and unauthorised transactions should be reported promptly with the request ID and bank/UPI reference. Do not share PINs or OTPs. BattleForge should acknowledge a complaint, investigate against transaction records and provide a written outcome. Statutory rights and bank/UPI dispute channels remain unaffected.

Request route

Submit a ticket in the app under Support and include the tournament name, player ID, request ID and a short explanation. Do not post personal payment information publicly.

How to complain

  1. Open an in-app support ticket. Select the closest category: account, payment, withdrawal, tournament, result/cheating report, privacy or safety.
  2. Include your BattleForge player ID, tournament/request ID, relevant dates and a clear description. Share only redacted evidence; never share passwords, OTPs or UPI PINs.
  3. We aim to acknowledge within 2 working days and provide an update or outcome within 15 calendar days. If more time is required, we will explain why and give an expected update date. These are proposed service targets and do not replace any shorter statutory deadline.
  4. For a match-result dispute, submit it within the deadline shown on that tournament's page. The tournament team should preserve evidence and record the decision and reason.
