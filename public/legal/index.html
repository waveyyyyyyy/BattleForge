01 · Your information

Privacy policy

BattleForge respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is used when you create an account, join tournaments, use wallet features or contact support.

Information we collect

  • Account details: sign-in email and authentication identifier, username, BattleForge Player ID, account creation details and selected in-app avatar.
  • Game activity: Free Fire in-game name, tournament entries, team and match participation, results, kills, rankings, match history and referral information.
  • Wallet and payment details: deposit and withdrawal amounts, request status, UPI IDs and account-holder name supplied by you, payment references, wallet transactions and relevant review records. Never provide your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or password.
  • Support and safety: messages, complaints, match evidence, reports, moderation actions and account-security records.
  • Technical information: device/platform details, push-notification token, app diagnostics and security logs needed to operate the service.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; identify players; operate tournaments, leaderboards and referrals; maintain accurate wallet records; review payment requests; send match, account and service notifications; investigate disputes and fraud; enforce fair-play rules; improve reliability; and comply with applicable requirements.

When information is shared

Information is shared only when needed to provide the service, protect users or comply with law. Service providers may process relevant data for database and authentication hosting (Supabase), object storage (Cloudflare), push notifications (Firebase), Google sign-in, and payment processing or banking selected for a transaction. Authorised BattleForge administrators may access information needed for their assigned duties. Tournament results, usernames and rankings may be visible to other players. We do not sell personal information.

Storage, security and retention

BattleForge uses access controls and technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect account and transaction information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to operate accounts and tournaments, maintain transaction history, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet applicable obligations. Backup copies may remain for a limited period after an account or record is changed.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage notification permissions in your device settings. You may contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible personal information. We may need to verify your account and may retain records that are required for security, financial reconciliation, fraud prevention or dispute handling.

Children and account safety

Do not create an account if you are not legally eligible to use the service in your location. Keep your login credentials and OTPs private. Notify support if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

02 · Using BattleForge

Terms of service

By creating an account or using BattleForge, you agree to these terms and the fair-play rules. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

Accounts

  • Provide accurate information and use an account that belongs to you.
  • Keep your sign-in method secure. You are responsible for activity performed through your account, except where unauthorised access is caused by our failure to take reasonable safeguards.
  • Do not impersonate another person, create duplicate accounts to abuse promotions, sell or transfer accounts, manipulate referrals or interfere with another player's account.
  • BattleForge may ask you to verify account details where reasonably necessary for security, payment review or dispute handling.

Tournaments

Each tournament page or match detail screen sets out the format, entry requirements, schedule, player capacity, scoring, prize details and organiser instructions. Check those details before joining. Tournament availability and schedules may change for operational, technical, safety or integrity reasons.

Coins and wallet

Coins are in-app units used for eligible BattleForge features. The app maintains separate records for deposited credits, promotional rewards and match earnings. Deposited credits and promotional rewards may have different usage conditions from match earnings; the applicable conditions are shown in the relevant wallet or reward flow. Coins are not a bank account, deposit account or general-purpose currency and cannot be transferred between users unless a feature expressly allows it.

Deposit requests are manually reviewed and are credited only after verification. Withdrawal requests are subject to eligibility checks, the minimum and fee displayed in the app at the time of request, and manual processing. A submitted request is not confirmation that a payment has been completed. Never share payment PINs or OTPs with anyone.

Results, prizes and corrections

Match results are based on the scoring rules displayed for that tournament and the evidence available to the organiser. Results may be held for review where there is a credible dispute, suspected manipulation, missing evidence or a technical error. If a published result contains an error, BattleForge may correct it and record the reason. Eligible rewards are credited after results are verified and published.

Availability and changes

We may update app features, tournament schedules, these terms or operational processes. Material changes will be communicated in the app or through the available contact channels. We may temporarily restrict access to maintain security, investigate abuse, comply with law or protect the integrity of a match.

Liability

BattleForge is provided on an as-available basis. To the extent permitted by law, BattleForge is not responsible for interruptions caused by third-party networks, game servers, device failures or events outside its reasonable control. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.

03 · Competitive integrity

Fair-play rules

  • Use the correct Free Fire in-game name registered for the match.
  • Join the correct room on time and follow the organiser's match instructions.
  • Cheats, hacks, exploits, collusion, deliberate teaming outside the format, match fixing, false score reports and harassment are prohibited.
  • Do not disrupt lobbies, impersonate players, abuse bugs or attempt to access another player's account.
  • Provide genuine evidence when reporting a result or conduct issue. Do not alter screenshots or submit misleading information.
  • BattleForge may review reports, request clarification, withhold disputed results during investigation, correct verified errors, or apply proportionate restrictions for rule violations.

Where practical, players will be given an opportunity to explain a disputed incident before a final moderation decision.

04 · Payments and changes

Cancellation, postponement & refund policy

If BattleForge cancels a tournament

If a tournament is cancelled by BattleForge before it begins, the entry amount paid for that tournament will be returned to the player's eligible wallet balance. If a match is postponed, the player may choose to remain registered for the revised schedule or request a return of the entry amount where the app provides that option.

If a player cancels or cannot attend

Once a player joins a tournament, cancellation and refund eligibility depends on the tournament status and the cancellation option shown in the app. A player who misses a match, joins the wrong room or is disqualified for a verified rule violation is generally not eligible for an entry refund, except where required by applicable law or where the organiser determines that a refund is appropriate.

Payment errors

If you paid but your deposit is not reflected, submit the payment reference and the exact amount through the wallet request flow. Do not submit a payment screenshot as proof of a transaction that did not occur. Duplicate, incorrect or unverified payments are reviewed individually. If a withdrawal is rejected, the eligible amount is returned to the relevant wallet balance according to the wallet rules.

How to request help

Contact support with your BattleForge Player ID, tournament name or transaction reference, amount and a short description. Never include your UPI PIN, OTP or password.

05 · We can help

Support, complaints & disputes

For account access, payment checks, privacy requests, tournament disputes or complaints, contact the BattleForge support team through BattleForge Support on Telegram.

Include your Player ID and relevant match or payment reference so the team can locate the issue. We aim to acknowledge support requests promptly and provide an update after reviewing the relevant records. Urgent account-security concerns should be reported as soon as possible.
