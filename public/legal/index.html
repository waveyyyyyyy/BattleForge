1. Privacy policy

BattleForge Arena ("BattleForge", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy of players and handles information to operate the app, tournaments, player accounts, wallet requests and support services.

Information we collect

  • Account details: sign-in email and authentication identifier, username, BattleForge Player ID, account creation details and the avatar selected from the in-app collection.
  • Game and tournament details: Free Fire in-game name, tournament registrations, match participation, results, placements, kills, leaderboard records and referral information.
  • Wallet and payment details: wallet balances and ledger entries, deposit and withdrawal requests, payment references, UPI IDs and account-holder name supplied for payout processing, request status and relevant review notes.
  • Support and safety details: support messages, complaints, match disputes, reports, moderation actions, warnings and account restrictions.
  • Technical details: device/platform information, push-notification token, app diagnostics and security logs needed to keep the service working.

How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts; identify players; operate registrations, matches and leaderboards; maintain wallet and transaction records; review deposits and withdrawals; administer referral rewards; send service and match notifications; provide support; investigate fraud, cheating and disputes; improve app reliability; and comply with applicable requirements.

When information is shared

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate BattleForge, including Supabase for authentication and database services, Cloudflare for configured object storage, Firebase services for push notifications, and Google services when Google sign-in is used. Payment information may also be handled by the UPI app, bank or payment service selected by the player. Authorised BattleForge administrators may access information needed for their assigned duties. Information may be disclosed where required by law or to protect players, the service and its security.

We do not sell players' personal information. We do not ask players to share UPI PINs, card PINs, passwords or one-time passcodes with support staff.

Public and player-visible information

Some information, such as a username, selected avatar, Player ID, tournament participation, match results and leaderboard position, may be visible to other players inside the app so that tournaments and community features can work. Do not use a password, phone number or other private information as your username.

Security and storage

We use access controls and technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss or misuse. Access to administrative and financial information is restricted according to assigned permissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

We keep account, tournament, wallet, payment, safety and support records for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain accurate records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet applicable requirements. Backups and security logs may remain for a limited period after a record is changed or deleted.

Your choices and requests

You may contact support to request access to or correction of eligible account information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify that you control the account before responding. Certain transaction, security or dispute records may need to be retained where permitted or required. You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

Children and account eligibility

BattleForge is intended for users who are legally eligible to use the app and participate in the relevant tournaments. Do not create an account or submit another person's information if you are not permitted to do so. Tournament-specific age and location restrictions must be followed.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact BattleForge Support through t.me/battleforgeff. Do not post personal payment details publicly.

2. Terms of service and fair-play rules

By creating an account or using BattleForge, you agree to follow these terms, the instructions shown on each tournament page and applicable laws and game rules. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

Accounts

  • Provide accurate information and use only an account you control.
  • Keep your sign-in credentials, passwords and OTPs private. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account, except where applicable law provides otherwise.
  • Do not impersonate another player, sell or transfer your account, create duplicate accounts to misuse offers, or manipulate referrals.
  • Keep your username and Free Fire in-game name appropriate and consistent with the tournament instructions.

Tournaments and participation

Each tournament may specify its game mode, entry requirements, schedule, map, team format, player capacity, scoring, room instructions, prizes and result process. Read those details before joining. A registration is subject to available slots, eligibility and the tournament's published rules.

Fair play and conduct

  • Cheating, hacks, exploits, collusion, match fixing, false score submissions, deliberate disruption, harassment and abusive conduct are prohibited.
  • Follow organiser instructions and use the correct in-game name and team format.
  • BattleForge may review reports, request relevant evidence, correct clear record errors, hold disputed results or restrict accounts where there is credible evidence of rule violations or abuse.
  • Where a decision affects a match result or player balance, the reason should be recorded and a support route made available.

Coins, wallet and payments

Coins are the in-app unit used to display wallet value and tournament activity. The wallet may show separately recorded deposits and match earnings. The app displays applicable deposit and withdrawal requirements, fees and request steps before a player submits a request. Deposit requests are subject to transaction verification. Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed manually; submission does not mean a payout has been completed. The current app settings include a minimum deposit of ₹30, a minimum withdrawal request of ₹100 and a 2% withdrawal processing fee.

Never share a UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or account password with anyone claiming to represent BattleForge. A payment screenshot by itself does not establish that a transfer has been received. Keep your UPI transaction reference for support enquiries.

Results, rewards and corrections

Match results and rewards are processed using the tournament's published scoring and result rules. Results may remain pending while they are checked. If a clear data-entry or calculation error is found, BattleForge may correct the record and explain the correction. Players should use the in-app support route to report an issue.

Availability and changes

We may update, suspend or discontinue features for maintenance, security, operational or legal reasons. We may update these terms from time to time. The latest version will be made available on this policy page.

Account restrictions

We may temporarily restrict or close an account where reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, cheating, abuse, security incidents or violations of these terms, or to comply with law. Players may contact support to ask for a review.

3. Cancellation, postponement and refund policy

When BattleForge cancels a tournament

If BattleForge cancels a tournament before it begins, affected registered players will be informed and the applicable entry amount will be returned or reversed through the payment or wallet method available for that transaction. The support team may request the tournament ID and payment reference to verify the request.

When a tournament is postponed

If a tournament is rescheduled, players will be informed of the revised schedule. If a player cannot participate because of the change, they may contact support before the revised match begins to request the remedy applicable to that tournament.

Player cancellation

Whether a player can cancel a registration or receive a refund depends on the cancellation cut-off and conditions displayed on the relevant tournament page. Once a match has started or a slot has been used, a refund may not be available except where required by the published tournament rules or applicable law.

Interrupted or disputed matches

If a match is interrupted, compromised or cannot be fairly completed, the organiser may pause result publication while the incident is reviewed. Depending on the facts and published rules, the outcome may include a replay, cancellation, correction or applicable refund. The decision should be communicated to affected players.

Payment errors and duplicate transfers

Report duplicate payments, incorrect credits, failed transfers or unauthorised transactions through in-app support or BattleForge Support. Include your Player ID, request ID and UPI transaction reference. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP. You may also contact your bank or UPI app using its official dispute process.

4. Complaints, support and match disputes

For account access, payment, withdrawal, tournament, result, conduct or privacy issues, contact BattleForge Support through the in-app support feature or t.me/battleforgeff.

What to include

  • Your BattleForge Player ID and the tournament or payment request ID, if applicable.
  • The date and a short explanation of the issue.
  • Relevant screenshots or transaction references with sensitive details hidden.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or full card details. The support team may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership and investigate the issue. We aim to acknowledge support requests within 2 working days and provide an update or outcome within 15 calendar days. Complex cases may take longer; where possible, we will explain the reason and provide an update.

Match-result disputes

Submit a result dispute through the match's available dispute option and include the match ID, the issue and supporting evidence. Submit it within any deadline shown on the tournament page. Results may be held for review while the dispute is investigated.
