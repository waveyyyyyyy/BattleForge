2.5 Wallet, coins and balance categories

Coins are the in-app unit used to display wallet value and tournament activity. The wallet maintains separate categories. Deposited balance is intended for eligible tournament entry fees and is not withdrawable as cash. Match earnings credited after results are verified may be used for eligible entry fees and may be requested for withdrawal, subject to the app's rules, verification, minimums, fees, legal eligibility and available processing arrangements. Promotional, referral, streak or other bonus credits may be restricted to entry fees and may be non-withdrawable; the applicable restriction will be shown when the reward is offered or credited.

Wallet balances and transaction history are maintained in BattleForge's records. A displayed balance may be adjusted to correct a verified duplicate, failed, reversed, erroneous or fraudulent transaction, or to implement a properly documented result correction. We will maintain an appropriate transaction record for material adjustments. Do not treat a pending request as a completed credit or payout.

2.6 Deposits and payment verification

The minimum deposit currently displayed in the app is ₹30. Follow the in-app instructions and pay only to the UPI ID or QR code displayed in the official deposit flow. Enter the exact amount, your paying UPI ID and the UTR/reference requested by the app, then submit the confirmation. The confirmation checkbox is your declaration that you have made the payment; it does not itself prove receipt.

Deposits are manually verified. A request may remain pending for up to 24 hours under normal operating conditions, and processing may take longer due to bank, UPI network, provider or verification delays. A deposit is credited only after the transaction is verified. Screenshots alone are not conclusive proof of a successful transfer. Do not pay an account or UPI ID supplied in unofficial messages, and never disclose your UPI PIN or OTP.

2.7 Withdrawals and processing fees

The minimum withdrawal request currently displayed in the app is ₹100. Withdrawals are available only from eligible withdrawable earnings, not from deposited or restricted promotional balances. You must provide the exact account-holder name and a valid UPI ID and may be asked for reasonable verification information. The current app fee is 2% of the requested withdrawal amount; the net payout is calculated after that fee and the app should display the applicable amount before submission.

Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed manually. Submitting a request does not mean it has been approved or paid. Under normal operating conditions, review and processing may take up to 24 hours, but bank, UPI network, provider, compliance or verification delays may extend this period. A request may be held or rejected where details are invalid, ownership cannot be verified, the balance is not withdrawable, a transaction is under review or the request appears fraudulent. If rejected, the eligible amount will be handled according to the wallet ledger and the reason communicated where practicable.

2.8 Cancellation, tournament changes and refunds

Player cancellation: A player may request cancellation before a match only if the relevant tournament listing or an in-app cancellation control expressly allows it. If no cancellation option or refund promise is displayed, registration should be treated as committed once the entry fee has been successfully deducted. A player who does not attend, joins late, enters the wrong room, supplies incorrect team details or is disqualified for a rule breach is not automatically entitled to a refund, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

Cancellation by BattleForge: If BattleForge cancels a tournament before it begins, the affected eligible entry fee will ordinarily be returned to the relevant wallet category or otherwise reversed through the original payment route where appropriate and technically available. The refund will not convert a non-withdrawable deposit or bonus into withdrawable earnings. If a tournament is postponed or materially changed, we will notify affected players where reasonably practicable and explain any available option or remedy before the rescheduled match begins.

Interrupted, invalid or compromised matches: If a match cannot be fairly completed due to a technical failure, organiser error, game-server issue, force majeure event or suspected integrity breach, we may pause result publication and investigate. Depending on the circumstances and the published tournament rules, the remedy may include continuing, replaying, rescheduling, cancelling the match, correcting results or returning eligible entry fees. A refund is not automatic solely because a player experiences an individual device, network or connectivity issue.

Payment errors and duplicate payments: If you believe you paid twice, paid the wrong amount, were debited without a corresponding credit, or received an incorrect wallet entry, contact support with your Player ID, request ID, date, amount and UTR/reference. We will investigate against available transaction records. You may also raise a transaction complaint through your UPI app or bank's official channels.

Refund timing: Once a refund or reversal is approved, the time for the amount to appear depends on the wallet, bank, UPI network and payment provider. We will communicate the approved method and any available reference. No provision in this section limits a right that cannot lawfully be excluded.
