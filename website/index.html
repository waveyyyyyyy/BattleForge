08 / FIELD GUIDE

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Which games does BattleForge support?

BattleForge supports Free Fire and many more games. Available games and tournaments are shown inside the app.

Which formats are available?

Solo, Duo and Squad formats are supported. Tournaments open for registration are shown in the app.

How are rewards calculated?

Rewards follow each tournament's published scoring and verification rules. Check the tournament page before joining.

How do wallet requests work?

Deposit and withdrawal requests are reviewed by the BattleForge team. You can follow request status and transaction history inside the app. All requests are subject to the rules and verification checks shown in the app.

How do I contact support?

Message official BattleForge support on Telegram.

Is BattleForge affiliated with game publishers?

BattleForge is an independent tournament platform. Game titles, marks and assets belong to their respective owners.

Account, payment or tournament questions? Talk to the team.
