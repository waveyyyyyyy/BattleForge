WE GOT YOU.

A few useful details before you step into the arena.

Which game does BattleForge support?

BattleForge is currently focused on Garena Free Fire tournaments.

Which tournament formats are available?

Solo, Duo and Squad formats are supported. The tournaments currently open for registration are shown in the app.

How are rewards calculated?

Rewards follow the scoring and verification rules published for each tournament. The current Solo example is ₹11 per verified kill with a ₹12 entry fee. Check the specific tournament page before joining.

Can I withdraw deposited credits?

No. Deposited credits are for tournament entry fees and are not withdrawable. Eligible match earnings may be withdrawn subject to the app's withdrawal rules and verification.

How do I contact support?

Reach the BattleForge support team through our official Telegram support.

Is BattleForge affiliated with Garena?

No. BattleForge is an independent community tournament platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Garena.
