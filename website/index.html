GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Which game does BattleForge support?

BattleForge is currently focused on Garena Free Fire tournaments.

Which tournament formats are available?

The app supports Solo, Duo and Squad formats. Availability depends on the tournaments published in the app.

How are rewards calculated?

Rewards are based on verified match results and the rules displayed for the specific tournament. The current Solo example is ₹11 per verified kill; always review the in-app details before joining.

Can I withdraw deposited wallet credits?

Deposited credits are for tournament entry fees and are not withdrawable. Eligible match earnings may be withdrawn subject to the app's withdrawal rules and verification.

Where can I get help?

Contact the BattleForge support team through our official Telegram support.
